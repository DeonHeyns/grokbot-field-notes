# Provenance

This fork adapts the MIT-licensed
[Grok Bot Field Notes](https://github.com/unicodef1wn/grokbot-field-notes) by
unicodef1wn for ChatGPT Work and Codex.

The starting revision in this fork was
[`02780c04ef5f412b28573bce6f82afd15d195d1f`](https://github.com/DeonHeyns/grokbot-field-notes/tree/02780c04ef5f412b28573bce6f82afd15d195d1f).
That immutable revision contains the original README, all 69 role cards and all
nine workshop narratives. Original author and speaker attributions remain there.

The PDFs in [guide/](guide/README.md) and three day-by-day notes in
[notes/](notes/README.md) are preserved. The original product snapshot, economics
and failure log have moved into `notes/grok-*.md` with historical labels.
Their prices, throughput and product behavior are not OpenAI claims.

The active README, AGENTS.md, agent guides, role cards, playbooks and generated
ports are adaptations. They retain narrow ownership, canonical sources, bounded
authority, verifiable results and fewer unnecessary recurring runs. Product
mappings are documented in [reference/MIGRATION.md](reference/MIGRATION.md).

Official documentation was reviewed on **24 October 2026**. This date records a
documentation review, not live validation of every account, plugin or workflow.
See [sources](reference/SOURCES.md). The pack contains no production agent service
and creates no scheduled tasks by itself.

The original [MIT licence](LICENSE) is unchanged.
